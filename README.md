# PUBUILD TECHNOLOGIES INC. - Government Server Solutions Website

A professional website designed exclusively for US government agencies and federal departments.

## Overview

PUBUILD TECHNOLOGIES INC. specializes in providing comprehensive server building, equipment installation, and manufacturing services exclusively for US government agencies. This website serves as the primary interface for government procurement and contractor information.

## Features

### 🏛️ Government-Focused Design
- Professional blue color scheme representing trust and government authority
- Clean, accessible layout meeting government website standards
- Clear messaging emphasizing government-only services

### 🔧 Core Services
- **Server Building & Assembly**: Custom server construction with government-grade components
- **Equipment Installation**: Professional installation in secure government facilities
- **Manufacturing**: In-house capabilities for specialized government requirements
- **Custom Design**: Tailored solutions meeting specific agency requirements
- **Security Integration**: Advanced security protocols and compliance measures
- **Support & Maintenance**: Ongoing technical support for government infrastructure

### 🛡️ Compliance & Certifications
- FISMA (Federal Information Security Management Act) compliance
- FedRAMP authorization for cloud solutions
- Security clearances for personnel
- Full contract compliance with federal acquisition regulations

### 📋 Government Procurement
- Active participation in SAM.gov (System for Award Management)
- GSA Schedule contracts for streamlined procurement
- IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity) contracts
- Competitive bidding on federal opportunities
- Small business set-aside program participation
